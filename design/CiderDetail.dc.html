LEGENDARY
DRY

Crisp, Dry & Champagne-Like

In homage to America’s hard cider history and the larger-than-life Captain Stormalong, our flagship cider is named “Legendary Dry”. Made with a unique blend of ‘bittersweet’ cider apples which impart a tannic finish and ‘champagne-like’ character. A British inspired cider with an American take. Less than 3 grams of sugar per can.

MADE WITH HEIRLOOM
BITTERSWEET APPLES

The mission of our Rare Apple Series is to highlight the virtues of extraordinary heirloom apple varieties and their exquisite transformation into distinctive cider. We aim to increase awareness of the diversity of apple cultivars and show what the right apples can do.

Less than 3 grams of sugar per can.
