Why this one: it is B — same slab face, same bone and gold, same double rules and stars — reorganised around the two jobs that actually matter for a brand sold through distribution: tell people what the cider is, then tell them where it is. No cart, no prices. The top is the front-page mosaic — one lead and three secondaries, all on screen at once — with the trophy case beneath it. Twenty-six medals across eight ciders is the strongest proof you have and it currently lives as a bullet list on detail pages; it also barely ever changes, so it earns the slot without becoming a maintenance job. Tasting notes and specs on every tile, the sweetness scale promoted to a guide of its own, the Rare Apple Series run as dated releases, and the locator given the whole bottom of the page in three flavours: take it home, drink it there, come to us. The trade: it lives or dies on a real stockist dataset. Everything else here you already have — the tasting notes just need writing.
