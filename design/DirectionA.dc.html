Our one rule
Eight GLINTCAP medals and two SF Chronicle silvers say it holds up.
Why this one: cider sits with wine and food, not beer. A paper ground, a high-contrast serif and an index instead of a card grid put Stormalong on the restaurant list rather than the shelf. The trade: it walks away from the nautical folklore entirely, and the can art — which is loud — has to be handled carefully against quiet pages.
