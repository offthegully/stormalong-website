★ Hover or tap any panel to open it — nothing rotates by itself ★
Good at: giving the lead real space without hiding the others. All four are on screen and named at all times — the closed panels are labelled and carry their can, so nobody has to guess what is behind them. The reader opens what they want; nothing advances on a timer.
Costs: it needs a real interaction to pay off, and the three closed panels get a fraction of the attention the open one does. Caps out at four. On a phone the shutter has to become a stack, so you are building two layouts.
